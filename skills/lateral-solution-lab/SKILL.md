# Lateral Solution Lab

Primary journey: **Design**

Use this only after the problem and constraints are understandable.

1. Express the central contradiction: what must improve and what currently gets worse.
2. Explore analogies from three unrelated domains and identify the transferable mechanism, not surface aesthetics.
3. Invert one assumption, remove one dependency, and change the unit of value.
4. Produce a small set of genuinely different solution mechanisms.
5. Rank them by novelty, constraint fit, testability, and downside; convert the best one into a reversible experiment.

Avoid “creative” ideas that merely add a chatbot, dashboard, or extra approval step without eliminating real work.
