# Opportunity Design

Primary journey: **Design**

Build an opportunity thesis from evidence rather than from trend language.

1. Define the segment, unmet job, substitute, and structural change that makes a new approach possible.
2. Identify an unfair advantage: workflow position, trusted data, integration, distribution, or learning loop.
3. Draft several business-model and product-mechanism options.
4. Score each with the ForgeMind USP model: value, differentiation, data, trust, effort, and time-to-MVP.
5. Specify a falsifiable experiment, leading metric, kill criterion, and next delivery step.

Use `usp-ai-strategist` for detailed scoring and `innovation-delivery-lab` when the opportunity should become a governed execution contract.
