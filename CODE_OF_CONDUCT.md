# Code of Conduct

## Our standard

Be respectful, specific, and constructive. Welcome different backgrounds and experience levels. Critique ideas and evidence, not people. Do not harass, threaten, discriminate, publish another person's private information, or use sexualized or demeaning language.

## Scope and enforcement

This standard applies in repository issues, pull requests, discussions, reviews, and other project spaces. Report conduct problems privately through the repository maintainer's GitHub profile. Do not put sensitive details in a public issue.

Maintainers may edit or remove content, close contributions, limit participation, or ban accounts when behavior harms the community. Enforcement decisions should consider context, impact, recurrence, and opportunities to repair harm. Good-faith reports and appeals will be reviewed as capacity permits.
