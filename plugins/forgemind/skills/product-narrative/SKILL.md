# Product Narrative

Primary journey: **Design**

Make the audience and intended decision explicit. Preserve factual boundaries; do not invent customer outcomes or market proof.

1. Name the audience, their current tension, and the change they want.
2. Establish the stakes and the concrete obstacle without exaggeration.
3. Show the mechanism of change and the proof available today.
4. End with the desired action, remaining uncertainty, and next evidence step.
5. Adapt the same truth for a one-line value proposition, a product page, and a spoken story.

Prefer specific before-and-after moments over abstract claims. Route claims that need verification through the evidence workflow.
